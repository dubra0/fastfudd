Melano Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

What we collect

  • Order details: name, phone number, address, items, notes and prices.
  • Account: email, name and mobile number. Gender and date of birth are optional. Passwords are stored hashed, and you can delete your account inside the app.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose it, we receive only your name, email and profile picture from Google.
  • Sign in with WhatsApp: if you choose it, your mobile number receives a one-time code by WhatsApp (or SMS). The code is sent through our messaging provider, OTPIQ, which receives only your number and the code.
  • Profile photo (optional): if you add one, it is stored on our server and shown only in your own profile. It is deleted when you delete your account.
  • Customer location: only if you send it or pick it on the map.
  • Driver location: during a delivery, so the customer can follow the order.
  • Chat messages: messages you exchange with the driver are stored with the order.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, we store your phone's push address and your language. It is removed when you log out or delete your account.

Why we use it

Only to prepare and deliver your order and to contact you about it. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

  • The restaurant owner and delivery drivers.
  • Supabase, which stores the data.
  • Google, for sign-in, if you choose it.
  • Your phone's push service (Google, Apple or Mozilla), to deliver notifications. The notification content is encrypted.
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri for maps and satellite images, and OSRM for route calculation (driver and customer location).

When you delete your account

Your account, profile and notification subscriptions are deleted. We keep only a one-way hash of your email address, not the address itself, so the same email cannot be used to open a new account. Order records stay for the restaurant's accounts, but are no longer linked to you.
