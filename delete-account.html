Delete your Melano account

In the app

  1. Open the app and log in.
  2. Tap 👤 Profile at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

By email

If you cannot log in, write from your account's email address to: aeadnajm33@gmail.com

Your account cannot be deleted while you have a pending or active order.

What is deleted

Your account and profile (email, name, mobile, gender and date of birth) are deleted, along with your phones' notification subscriptions. Order records stay for the restaurant's accounts, but are no longer linked to your account.

We keep only a one-way hash of your email address, not the address itself, so the same email cannot be used to open a new account.
